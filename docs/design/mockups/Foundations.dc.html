Analyte design system · v1.0 · analyte.css

Foundations

Three layers of tokens. Primitives (--p-*) are the raw palette and are never used by components. Semantic tokens say what a colour is for, and are the only colours components use, so light and dark both come for free. Scales cover type, spacing, shape and motion.

  • Status first, details after
  • Never colour alone: icon + word
  • Numbers in mono, in columns
